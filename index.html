Live · Chrome Web Store

Card Price Checker for Whatnot

A Chrome extension that reads the Pokémon card a seller is holding up on a Whatnot live stream and shows its market price, so you can tell in seconds whether the bid is a deal.

  • ~95%ID accuracy across 10+ stream setups
  • 0.1–0.7 sprice lookups, with caching
  • 84automated tests with mocked APIs
  • Runs Chrome's on-device AI (Gemini Nano) when it can and falls back to a hosted card-ID service, so no API key is needed.
  • Auto-scan watches the video and fires once a new card settles, ignoring foil sparkle, hand sway and card backs.
  • English and Japanese prices, plus exact-grade prices for PSA, CGC and BGS slabs.
JavaScript · Chrome Extensions (MV3) · Gemini Nano · Cloudflare Workers

Nodoff

A transit alarm for people who fall asleep on the bus. It sounds a loud, vibrating alarm as you approach your stop, even with the app closed or the phone locked.

  • 3,831stops from TheBus and Skyline data
  • 18nearest stops armed under iOS's 20-region limit
  • 4beta testers on TestFlight
  • Uses the phone's native geofencing instead of streaming GPS, so it barely touches the battery while you ride.
  • Re-picks which stops to watch every 2 km, and survives the app being closed or the phone restarting.
  • Added silent alerts after tester feedback.
Next.js · Capacitor · TypeScript · Postgres/Prisma · GTFS

Critter Clash

A multiplayer army battler. Six sides earn food, spend it calling in animal troops by supply drop, and fight over seven meadows. Hold more ground, earn more food, field a bigger army.

  • 6sides per match, filled by bots when short
  • 12animals, with 50 skins to hatch
  • 15 minrounds, last base standing wins
  • Game logic runs on the server so clients can't cheat. Every system is split into config, pure logic, server and client modules.
  • Lobby with ranked queue, loadouts, quests, leaderboards and saved progress.
